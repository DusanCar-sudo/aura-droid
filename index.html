The Gauntlet Loop

How the agent works — and proves it.

Every session runs the same disciplined loop, whether the agent lives on your PC or in Termux on the phone.

01

Read

Aura reads the codebase, maps dependencies, and understands the world it's entering before touching anything.

02

Plan

A strategy is formed — scoped, minimal, and aligned with your intention.

03

Execute

Changes are made surgically. No rewrites for the sake of rewrites.

04

Verify

The gauntlet fires: tests, checks, screenshots. If it doesn't pass, it doesn't ship.

05

Repeat

Failures are analyzed, not hidden. The loop runs until the work is genuinely done.
